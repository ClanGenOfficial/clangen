# Cruel Season Cards Style Guide

- Medieval tarot vibes
- Lineless, little to *no* shading. Flat shapes are preferred.
- Focus on strong shapes and silhouettes
- Repetition, mirroring, 
- Symbolism over realism
- Similar proportions and realism level as patrol sprites, though sightly more stylization is allowed. NOTE: *not* "cartoony", but stylized.
- Work WITH the pixelization, not against. Allow shapes to take on the straight diagonals and lines of pixel art, rather than trying to force organic shapes. Curves can enhance the design, but don't fight to obscure the pixeled nature of it.


- Strict color palette
- Each card is allowed *one* accent color dictated by its category
